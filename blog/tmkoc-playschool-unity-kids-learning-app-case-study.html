Case Study: Building TMKOC Playschool, a Kids Learning App in Unity

How I built the TMKOC Playschool kids learning app from scratch in Unity: registration and OTP login, student dashboard, REST APIs, Addressables content downloads, localization and learning mini-games.

By Sachin Korde, Unity Developer, Pune 2 min read 15 Nov 2026
UnityC#REST APIAddressablesLocalizationE-learning

Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah (TMKOC) is India's No. 1 TV serial, and its characters are loved by families across the country. At Neela Mediatech I worked on official TMKOC mobile games, and the biggest of them was TMKOC Playschool Kids Learning: an e-learning app that teaches young children through games featuring the characters they already know.

I built Playschool from scratch in Unity, from the very first screen to the finished app on Google Play.

The goal

The app had to do two jobs at once. For children, it needed to feel like playing, not studying. For parents and teachers, it needed real structure: student accounts, progress tracking and reports they could rely on.

What I built

  • Registration and OTP authentication: a sign-up and login flow connected to the backend through REST APIs.
  • Student dashboard: a home screen where each child's data is fetched from the server, displayed and updated.
  • Addressables content downloads: learning content is downloaded from the server when it's needed, instead of being packed into the install.
  • Localization: the app is built so its text can be shown in more than one language.
  • Learning mini-games: I designed and built the Paint, ABCD Tracing and Puzzle games inside the app.
  • Student progress reports: modules that turn play sessions into progress reports for parents and educators.

Why Addressables mattered

A learning app keeps growing: new letters, new activities, new characters. If every piece of content ships inside the app, the download keeps getting bigger and every content change means a new store release. With Unity Addressables, content lives on the server and is loaded on demand. That keeps the first download smaller and lets the team add learning content without forcing every family to update the app.

Designing games for small hands

Games like ABCD Tracing and Paint are aimed at very young children, so the interaction has to be forgiving and the feedback has to be instant and joyful. A child tracing a letter should feel rewarded the moment they get it right. Building these mini-games taught me a lot about input handling, touch feedback and animation timing for an audience that can't read instructions yet.

Tech stack

Unity, C#, REST APIs, OTP authentication, Unity Addressables, Unity Localization, Android.

Result

TMKOC Playschool is live on Google Play. The same student-tracking features were also mirrored in a JavaScript web portal so the learning experience stays consistent across platforms.
