Game Development Company in Pune or a Freelance Unity Developer? How to Choose

Comparing game development companies in Pune with hiring a freelance Unity developer? A practical guide from a Pune game developer: when each makes sense, what to ask, and red flags to avoid.

By Sachin Korde, Unity Developer, Pune 3 min read 15 Nov 2026
PuneHiringGame StudiosFreelanceGuide

If you're searching for gaming companies in Pune or a game development company in Pune, you probably have a game to build and want the right team for it. Pune has a growing gaming and tech scene, and you can work with a full studio, a small agency or a freelance developer. As a game developer in Pune myself, here's an honest guide to choosing.

When a game development company makes sense

  • Big scope: you need a full team of programmers, artists, designers, QA and producers working together for many months.
  • Lots of original art: characters, environments and animation produced in-house.
  • One contract for everything: you want a single company responsible for design, development, testing and launch.

When a freelance Unity developer makes sense

  • A prototype or MVP: you want to test a game idea quickly before investing more.
  • Feature work on an existing game: new levels, events, UI, or a new game mode.
  • Optimization and polish: your game lags or its animations feel rough on real phones.
  • A specific skill: AR/VR, multiplayer card games, kids learning games or Unity–API integration.
  • Extra hands for your studio: a developer who can join your team and get up to speed fast.

Questions to ask any studio or developer

  • What have you shipped? Ask for Play Store or App Store links, not just screenshots.
  • What exactly was your role? "Worked on" can mean anything; ask what they personally built.
  • Who owns the source code? Make sure you get the full Unity project at the end.
  • How will we communicate? Agree on weekly builds or demos so you always see progress.
  • How do you test on real devices? Mobile games must be tested on low-end phones too, not just in the editor.

Red flags to watch for

  • No live games or demos to show.
  • Vague answers about who will actually write the code.
  • No plan for testing, bug fixing or post-launch updates.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pune a good city for game development?

Yes. Pune has a strong IT and engineering talent pool and a growing community of game developers, studios and startups, so it's a good place to find people for game projects.

Should I hire a game studio or a freelancer for my first game?

For a first game or prototype, a freelancer or a small team is often enough to validate the idea. A full studio makes more sense once the scope, art needs and budget are larger.

Can a freelance developer work with my in-house team in Pune?

Yes. A Pune-based developer can join your office for meetings or work alongside your designers and artists, and also work remotely.
