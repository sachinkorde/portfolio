Unity Beyond Games: 3D Digital Twins for Oil & Gas Pressure Testing (DPT)

How I built exact 3D replicas of BOP stacks, choke manifolds and MPD equipment in Unity and Blender for Aquila's Digital Pressure Testing platform, tested against real-time pressure data.

By Sachin Korde, Unity Developer, Pune 2 min read 15 Nov 2026
Unity 3DC#BlenderReal-time DataReactJS

Unity isn't only for games. At Aquila Digital Energy Solutions I worked on Digital Pressure Testing (DPT), a Unity-based platform that validates drilling-rig pressure tests in real time against the API STD 53 standard.

The domain in one minute

  • BOP (Blowout Preventer): a large stack of valves on top of a well that can seal it in an emergency. Every valve must be pressure-tested.
  • Choke Manifold: pipes and adjustable valves used to control pressure from the well.
  • MPD (Managed Pressure Drilling): a drilling method that controls well pressure very precisely.
  • Casing: the steel pipe cemented into the well; casing pressure tests prove it holds pressure without leaking.

My role

Every client rig is different. Whenever the company received a client's Choke Manifold, BOP or MPD structure, I turned it into an exact, interactive 3D replica inside the product.

  • From schematic to 3D: I converted engineering schematics into accurate 3D equipment in Unity.
  • Blender modeling: when a 3D model was missing, I built it myself in Blender.
  • Real-time data testing: I tested each new configuration against real-time pressure data before it was released to the client.
  • New features: I added a Casing test workflow and automated PDF report generation.
  • Web parity: I kept matching features in sync in the company's ReactJS web application.

Why game-dev skills transfer so well

A real-time 3D industrial tool needs the same things a game does: clean 3D assets, a smooth frame rate, clear interaction and solid architecture. The difference is that accuracy matters even more. A valve in the wrong place isn't a visual bug, it's wrong information for an engineer. Working on DPT made me much more careful about matching 3D content to real-world specifications.

Tech stack

Unity 3D, C#, Blender, real-time data integration, PDF report generation, ReactJS.
